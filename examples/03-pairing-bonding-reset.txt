# Posts 4 & 7 example: pair with bonding, reconnect on the bond, then reset both
# sides to a verified-clean state. Replace <PERIPHERAL_MAC>.
# Lesson from the field: a clean bond list is NOT proof of a healthy GATT client.
# Verify readiness by performing a real GATT operation, and keep ATR in your
# recovery path if a read fails while bonds look clean.

# clean slate on BOTH sides
[A] AT+GAPUNPAIR
[B] AT+GAPUNPAIR
[A] AT+GETBOND @expect No Bonded
[B] AT+GETBOND @expect No Bonded

# connect, pair with bonding, verify
[B] AT+ADVSTART @expect ADVERTISING
[A] AT+CENTRAL
[A] AT+GAPCONNECT=[0]<PERIPHERAL_MAC> @wait 5 @expect CONNECTED
[A] AT+GAPPAIR=BOND @wait 5 @expect BONDING SUCCESS
[A] AT+GETCONN @expect bonded=true
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECT @wait 2 @expect DISCONNECTED

# reconnect on the stored bond (no re-pairing) and confirm
[B] AT+ADVSTART @expect ADVERTISING
[A] AT+GAPCONNECT=[0]<PERIPHERAL_MAC> @wait 5 @expect CONNECTED
[A] AT+GETCONN @expect bonded=true
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECT @wait 2 @expect DISCONNECTED

# reset, verified on both sides
[A] AT+GAPUNPAIR
[B] AT+GAPUNPAIR
[A] AT+GETBOND @expect No Bonded
[B] AT+GETBOND @expect No Bonded
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
