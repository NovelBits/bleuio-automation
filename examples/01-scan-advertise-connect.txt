# Post 1 example: advertise a named peripheral, scan for it, connect, disconnect.
# Two BleuIO dongles. Replace <PERIPHERAL_MAC> below with dongle B's address:
#   python3 bleuio.py cmd "AT+GETMAC" --port <B's port>
# Find your ports with:  python3 bleuio.py find --all
# [A] = central/scanner, [B] = peripheral/advertiser.

# Known starting state. A dongle keeps its role, advertising and links between runs,
# and a peripheral resumes advertising on its own after a disconnect, which makes a
# later AT+CENTRAL answer ERROR. ERROR on these setup lines just means "nothing to do";
# the two AT+GAPSTATUS lines are the real check.
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
[B] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
@pause 1
[A] AT+ADVSTOP
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
[A] AT+CENTRAL
[B] AT+PERIPHERAL
[A] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Central role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising
[B] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Peripheral role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising

[A] ATI @expect BleuIO
[B] ATI @expect BleuIO

# B advertises as "SENSOR" (07=len, 09=Complete Local Name, then S E N S O R in hex)
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
[B] AT+ADVDATA=07:09:53:45:4E:53:4F:52 @expect OK
[B] AT+ADVSTART @expect ADVERTISING

# A scans and connects
[A] AT+CENTRAL
[A] AT+GAPSCAN=5 @wait 6.5 @expect SCAN COMPLETE
[A] ATASSN1 @expect Show Scan Name On
[A] AT+GAPCONNECT=[0]<PERIPHERAL_MAC> @wait 5 @expect CONNECTED
[A] AT+GETCONN @expect Client
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECT @wait 2 @expect DISCONNECTED
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
