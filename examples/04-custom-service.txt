# Post 8 example: build a programmable peer (a custom GATT service) in AT
# commands, then discover and read it from the central. Replace <PERIPHERAL_MAC>.
# GATTCREAD's completion event arrives asynchronously; @until reads until it lands.
# Before running: reboot both dongles (send ATR to each, then wait about 18 s for the
# ports to come back). From a fresh boot on FW 2.7.9.78 the custom service sits at 0024 and its
# value at 0026. Without the reboot, and on older firmware such as 2.5.0, it can land after the
# Device Information service instead (0031, value 0033), and the reads below return the wrong
# characteristic. Check the GAPCONNECT discovery output and change 0026 if yours differs.

# Known starting state. A dongle keeps its role, advertising and links between runs,
# and a peripheral resumes advertising on its own after a disconnect, which makes a
# later AT+CENTRAL answer ERROR. ERROR on these setup lines just means "nothing to do";
# the two AT+GAPSTATUS lines are the real check.
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
[B] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
@pause 1
[A] AT+ADVSTOP
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
[A] AT+CENTRAL
[B] AT+PERIPHERAL
[A] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Central role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising
[B] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Peripheral role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising

[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICERESET
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=0=UUID=ee6ec068-7447-4045-9fd0-593f3ba3c2ee
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=UUID=018f55d9-d747-4045-9fd0-593f3ba3c2aa
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=PROP=RN
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=PERM=R
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=LEN=20
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=VALUE=lab temp 21.5
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE @expect lab temp 21.5
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICESTART
[B] AT+ADVSTART @expect ADVERTISING

# central connects, discovers, reads (handle 0026 on FW 2.7.9.78 -- confirm via AT+GETSERVICES)
[A] AT+GAPCONNECT=[0]<PERIPHERAL_MAC> @wait 9 @expect 018f55d9
[A] AT+GATTCREAD=0026 @wait 6 @until Value read @expect lab temp 21.5

# update the value on the peripheral, read the change on the central
[B] AT+CUSTOMSERVICE=1=VALUE=lab temp 24.9 @expect OK
[A] AT+GATTCREAD=0026 @wait 6 @until Value read @expect lab temp 24.9

[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECT @wait 2 @expect DISCONNECTED
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
