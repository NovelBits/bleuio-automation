# Post 2 example: exchange data over the Serial Port Service, and reproduce the
# no-subscriber ERROR on demand. Replace <PERIPHERAL_MAC>. 000D = the SPS TX
# characteristic value handle on firmware 2.7.9.78 (confirm with AT+GETSERVICES).

# Known starting state. A dongle keeps its role, advertising and links between runs,
# and a peripheral resumes advertising on its own after a disconnect, which makes a
# later AT+CENTRAL answer ERROR. ERROR on these setup lines just means "nothing to do";
# the two AT+GAPSTATUS lines are the real check.
[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
[B] AT+GAPDISCONNECTALL @wait 1
@pause 1
[A] AT+ADVSTOP
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
[A] AT+CENTRAL
[B] AT+PERIPHERAL
[A] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Central role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising
[B] AT+GAPSTATUS @expect Peripheral role @expect Not Connected @expect Not Advertising

[B] AT+ADVSTART @expect ADVERTISING
[A] AT+GAPCONNECT=[0]<PERIPHERAL_MAC> @wait 5 @expect CONNECTED

# The BleuIO central auto-subscribes to SPS TX during discovery, so this works:
[A] AT+NOTI @expect 000D
[B] AT+SPSSEND=hello from the peripheral @expect Sent
[A] @read 1.5 @expect hello from the peripheral

# Unsubscribe -> the peripheral's next send has no subscriber -> ERROR (by design)
[A] AT+CLEARNOTI=000D @expect 000e
[B] AT+SPSSEND=anyone listening? @expect ERROR

# Resubscribe -> it works again
[A] AT+SETNOTI=000D
[B] AT+SPSSEND=back online @expect Sent
[A] @read 1.5 @expect back online

[A] AT+GAPDISCONNECT @wait 2 @expect DISCONNECTED
[B] AT+ADVSTOP
